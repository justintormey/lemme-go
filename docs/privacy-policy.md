# LemmeGo Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

LemmeGo does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no
analytics, and no server. The app contains no networking code at all, so there
is nowhere for your information to go even if we wanted it.

## What LemmeGo stores, and where

Everything below is written to LemmeGo's own storage on your iPhone. None of it
leaves the device by any route the app controls.

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| The hardware ID of each NFC tag you register, plus the name you gave it | So the app can tell your tag apart from any other tag |
| Your active or most recent lock session: start time, length, and which tag started it | So a session survives the app being closed or the phone restarting |
| Your blocked-app selection | So the same apps are blocked next time |
| A record of each emergency unlock you use, with its timestamp | To enforce the five-per-week limit |

We do not record which apps you actually open, how long you use them, where you
are, or anything about who you are.

## Screen Time

LemmeGo uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (Family Controls and Managed Settings) to
block the apps you choose. Apple hands the app opaque tokens that stand in for
your selected apps. Those tokens are meaningless outside your device and cannot
be read back into app names by us or by anyone else.

LemmeGo requests individual authorization only, meaning you are restricting your
own phone. It does not enrol children, does not use Apple's DeviceActivity
monitoring, and never reads your actual usage.

## NFC

LemmeGo reads only the unique hardware ID that an NFC tag or contactless card
broadcasts. It never writes to a tag. If you register a contactless payment
card, be aware that the app reads only its ID; it has no ability to read
cardholder details, balances, or payment data, and no code that could.

## Backups

Because LemmeGo's data lives in normal app storage, it is included in your
iPhone backups if you back up your phone to iCloud or a computer. That backup is
governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own settings, not by us. Registered
tag IDs are stored without additional encryption.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. There are no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no
tracking, and no data brokers involved.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it, including any Screen Time
restrictions it had applied.

## Children

LemmeGo is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including
children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
