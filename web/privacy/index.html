LemmeGo

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

LemmeGo does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no server. The app contains no networking code at all, so there is nowhere for your information to go even if we wanted it.

What LemmeGo stores, and where

Everything below is written to LemmeGo's own storage on your iPhone. None of it leaves the device by any route the app controls.

Registered NFC tags
The hardware ID of each tag you register, plus the name you gave it, so the app can tell your tag apart from any other tag.
Your current lock session
Start time, length, and which tag started it, so a session survives the app being closed or the phone restarting.
Your blocked-app selection
So the same apps are blocked next time.
Emergency unlock records
A timestamp for each emergency unlock, used only to enforce the five-per-week limit.

We do not record which apps you actually open, how long you use them, where you are, or anything about who you are.

Screen Time

LemmeGo uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (Family Controls and Managed Settings) to block the apps you choose. Apple hands the app opaque tokens that stand in for your selected apps. Those tokens are meaningless outside your device and cannot be turned back into app names by us or by anyone else.

LemmeGo requests individual authorization only, meaning you are restricting your own phone. It does not enrol children, does not use Apple's DeviceActivity monitoring, and never reads your actual usage.

NFC

LemmeGo reads only the unique hardware ID that an NFC tag or contactless card broadcasts. It never writes to a tag.

If you register a contactless payment card, note that the app reads only its ID. It has no ability to read cardholder details, balances, or payment data, and contains no code that could.

Backups

Because LemmeGo's data lives in normal app storage, it is included in your iPhone backups if you back up to iCloud or to a computer. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own settings, not by us. Registered tag IDs are stored without additional encryption.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. There are no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no tracking, and no data brokers involved.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it, including any Screen Time restrictions it had applied.

Children

LemmeGo is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
